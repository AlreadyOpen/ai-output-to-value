# Corrections and independent review

Corrections are part of the publication process, not an embarrassment to hide.

## Report a problem

Please open a GitHub issue for:

- a factual error;
- a source that does not support the published claim;
- a qualification lost in summarisation;
- a broken or superseded source;
- an accessibility or usability problem;
- a broken internal link or build problem;
- wording that makes editorial interpretation look like a research finding.

When possible, include the page and sentence involved, the source or reasoning that shows the problem, the proposed correction, and whether other claims or pages are affected.

## Review status

The project separates structural checks from independent evidence review.

Automated checks can confirm that source IDs exist, claim records contain required fields, metadata types are valid, publication locators exist, and links resolve. They cannot by themselves establish that a source truly supports a published sentence.

Claim records therefore include:

- a reviewer or review process;
- a review date;
- an `independent_review_status`;
- for completed reviews, a structured `review_record` identifying the claim revision, source versions checked, method, finding, and disposition.

Independent review is actor-neutral. Human, AI, automated, specialist-toolchain, and hybrid review can all qualify when the process is sufficiently separate from the originating work and leaves an auditable record.

A required human sign-off under law, contract, policy, or professional rules is an authority/governance requirement rather than evidence of inherently better reasoning.
