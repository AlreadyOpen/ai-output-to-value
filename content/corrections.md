# Corrections and editorial review

Corrections are part of the publication process, not an embarrassment to hide.

## Report a problem

Please open a GitHub issue in this repository for:

- a factual error;
- a source that does not support the published claim;
- an important qualification that was lost in summarisation;
- a broken or superseded source;
- an accessibility or usability problem;
- a broken internal link or build problem;
- wording that makes an editorial interpretation look like a research finding.

When possible, include:

1. the page and sentence involved;
2. the source or reasoning that shows the problem;
3. the proposed correction;
4. whether other claims or pages are affected.

## Review status

The project separates automated publication checks from editorial evidence review.

Automated checks can confirm that source IDs exist, claim records contain required fields, structured data parses, and local links resolve.

They **cannot** establish that a paper, policy, vendor page, or dataset truly supports a published sentence. That remains an editorial review task.

Claim records in `data/claims.yml` therefore include a reviewer, review date, and human-review status.
