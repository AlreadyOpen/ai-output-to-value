From AI output to business value.

Buying an API key or subscription can unlock powerful capability. It does not automatically create a finished deliverable, a repeatable business capability, or a profitable product.

Use AI ambitiously. Keep the categories clear.

Three distinctions worth remembering

Access is not capability. A licence provides a tool, not automatically the people, process, context, or judgement to use it well.
Output is not completion. Generation may be only one stage of review, correction, integration, delivery, and support.
Activity is not value. More prompts, tokens, generated code, or licences are not business outcomes by themselves.

The core model

Do not collapse six different achievements into one.

Each stage answers a different business question. The model is not a maturity score: some prototypes should intentionally stop early, while higher-consequence uses need stronger evidence.

  1. 01

    Access

    We have a model, API, subscription, agent, or tool.

    Does not prove effective use.
  2. 02

    Output

    The system produced code, text, analysis, media, or another artefact.

    Does not prove correctness or fitness.
  3. 03

    Deliverable

    The result meets defined acceptance criteria for its intended use.

    Does not prove repeatability.
  4. 04

    Capability

    People, process, context, tooling, ownership, and support exist around it.

    Does not prove a valuable outcome.
  5. 05

    Outcome

    Something meaningful changed: time, quality, throughput, service, risk, revenue, or another measure.

    Does not prove the gain exceeds cost.
  6. 06

    Value

    The outcome is worth the full cost, risk, and trade-offs of achieving it.

    Does not imply every task should use the same approach.

Do not mix the terms

Vibe coding, AI-assisted work, and AI slop describe different things.

One describes a way of working, one describes AI involvement, and one is a quality judgement. Treating them as synonyms makes useful management discussion harder.

Way of working

Vibe coding

Prompt-driven software creation and rapid iteration, sometimes with limited inspection of the generated implementation.

Useful for exploration and prototyping. The key question is what evidence is needed when intended use changes.

Workflow description

AI-assisted work

AI contributes to the work while people define the task, evaluate the result, and decide whether it is fit for use.

AI-assisted does not mean low quality, and it does not certify high quality.

Quality problem

AI slop / workslop

Output that appears finished but lacks enough substance, correctness, context, or usefulness for the person who must use it.

The business issue is often hidden downstream thinking, checking, or cleanup—not AI authorship by itself.

Count the whole job

A faster draft is useful. It is not the same metric as a faster completed workflow.

Measure preparation, generation, review, correction, integration, delivery, and support at the level that matters for the decision.

AI can reduce the total. It can also move work to another person or stage. Both effects should be visible.

Illustrative technical document Hours
Drafting 8→2
Review & correction 1→4
Integration / formatting 1→1
Total workflow 10→7

The drafting step is 75% faster. The whole illustrative workflow is 30% faster. Both statements can be true.

Use this in a meeting

Six questions before scaling an AI initiative.

  1. 1

    What exactly have we demonstrated?
    Separate a working demo from assumptions about readiness or demand.

  2. 2

    What remains to be checked or built before the intended use?
    Make the remaining work visible.

  3. 3

    Which work disappeared, and which work moved elsewhere?
    Look beyond generation time.

  4. 4

    Who owns verification, approval, operation, and support?
    Do not let responsibility hide between roles.

  5. 5

    Which business outcome are we trying to change?
    Name the measure before celebrating the activity.

  6. 6

    What evidence would justify scaling this?
    Decide what would count as success, failure, or uncertainty.

Beyond the API key

Use AI ambitiously. Verify what matters.

Prototype quickly. Automate work that can be automated. Let more people build useful things. Then measure the whole workflow, match assurance to consequence, make ownership visible, and judge success by outcomes that matter.
