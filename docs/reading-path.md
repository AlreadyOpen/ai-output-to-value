# Reading path

The repository contains a focused business guide plus broader analysis. A first-time reader should not have to read everything in order.

## Path 1 — first-time business reader

Read these in order:

1. [`START-HERE.md`](../START-HERE.md) — the core argument in about five minutes.
2. [`content/tool-access-vs-client-readiness.md`](../content/tool-access-vs-client-readiness.md) — the practical distinction between tool capability, job substance, and delivery capability.
3. [`content/executive-guide.md`](../content/executive-guide.md) — the fuller management guide, including workflow accounting, ownership, quality, and business value.

If those three pages answer the reader's question, they can stop there.

## Path 3 — evidence and editorial review

For contributors, reviewers, researchers, and readers who want to inspect the evidence system:

1. [`docs/evidence-policy.md`](evidence-policy.md) — evidence classes, claim statuses, scope, anecdotes, and corrections.
2. [`data/claims.yml`](../data/claims.yml) — claim-level traceability for launch-critical factual claims.
3. [`data/sources.yml`](../data/sources.yml) — core source register.
4. [`data/source-code-open-source-sources.yml`](../data/source-code-open-source-sources.yml) — open-source and technical-capability evidence.
5. [`data/corporate-role-value-sources.yml`](../data/corporate-role-value-sources.yml) — company-role and dynamic-capability evidence.

## Editorial rule

The home page and `START-HERE.md` should remain narrow:

> **We bought or gained access to AI. What have we actually achieved, what remains, and what evidence connects the output to business value?**

Broader material belongs in deeper reading unless it is necessary to answer that question directly.
