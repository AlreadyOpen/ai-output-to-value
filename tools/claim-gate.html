Claim gate

Use the ladder as a stop rule, not a score. Start with the decision, then require the weakest claim that is sufficient for that decision. Do not average strength at one claim level into another.

Gate ≠ truth. A gate result evaluates the supplied decision record. It is not an independent audit of the underlying system, measurement, or evidence.
